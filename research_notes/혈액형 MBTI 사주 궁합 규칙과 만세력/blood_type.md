# 혈액형 궁합표 (Blood Type Compatibility, Korean/Japanese popular lore)

Scope note: this covers the popular/entertainment lore (혈액형 성격설 / 血液型性格分類) only, not medical transfusion compatibility. Every source consulted (Korean and Japanese) states the lore has no scientific basis; the app should label it as 재미용. All numeric 0–100 scores in this file are **derived by the researcher** from the cited tables, as instructed.

## Key Question 1: For every ordered pair (남 × 여, 16 cells), what is the popular compatibility rating and one-line reason?

### Takeaway
Korean-circulated 혈액형 궁합표 are almost always a 남자 혈액형 × 여자 혈액형 16-cell grid with percentage scores; two independent Korean tables agree on the big picture (O남×A여 95% is the top cell, A남×O여 80–90%, B남×AB여 85–90%, B남×A여 and A남×B여 are the floor) but disagree by 20–30 points on mid-range cells, so no single canonical number set exists.

### Inferences
- Averaging the two Korean % tables per ordered cell gives a defensible "Korean consensus" directional score (남×여): A×A 73, A×B 38, A×O 85, A×AB 58, B×A 40, B×B 70, B×O 78, B×AB 88, O×A 95, O×B 68, O×O 53, O×AB 48, AB×A 53, AB×B 63, AB×O 40, AB×AB 80. These agree in rank with the Japanese Smartlog best-5 (O男A女, A男O女, B男O女, A男A女 all ≥70) and worst-5 (A男B女, B男A女, AB男O女 all ≤40), except for the B–AB pairs (see Gaps).
- The cells both Korean tables and the Japanese list agree on are the robust ones to surface in the app: O남×A여 (top), A남×O여 (high), B남×O여 (high), A남×A여 (solid), A남×B여 / B남×A여 (bottom), AB남×O여 (low), O남×O여 (mediocre, 주도권 다툼).

### Gaps
- No single "canonical" Korean 혈액형 궁합표 image could be identified as the most-shared one; the two full Korean tables found share the same 남×여 % format but differ by 20–30 points on 7 of 16 cells (e.g., B남×A여 20% vs 60%, AB남×A여 65% vs 40%, O남×O여 40% vs 65%).
- Disagreement on B–AB pairs: Korean tables rate B남×AB여 85–90% and AB남×B여 60–65%, whereas Smartlog's Japanese romance ranking lists AB男×B女 as worst #1 and B男×AB女 as worst #3 (while Smartlog's own symmetric section calls B×AB "low-stress"). I could not resolve which view dominates; the Korean view is recommended for a Korean app.
- Could not retrieve the full text of 横田晋大 (2013) 「血液型と相性(1)」 (repository PDF link expired), so Nomi Masahiko's original 1971 pair claims are not verified from a primary source — [shudo-u.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/2056](https://shudo-u.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/2056)

## Key Question 3: Are there commonly cited "best match" and "worst match" per type?

### Takeaway
Yes: O–A is the universally cited 찰떡궁합 (both directions, in Korean and Japanese sources), A–B is the universally cited 상극/"most effort needed", and AB–AB is cited as the best same-type pair; B's best match splits between O (Japanese) and AB (Korean).

### Cited Findings
- "O형과 A형은 최고의 궁합이라고 불릴 정도로 서로 끌어당기는 요소가 강하다" (O drawn to A's 차분·곧음, A to O's 활발·적극) — [m.cafe.daum.net/latindance/73e/16777](https://m.cafe.daum.net/latindance/73e/16777)

- A–O was the top pick (27.6%) in a Korean survey of best pairings — [kidd.co.kr/news/161658](https://kidd.co.kr/news/161658)
- Korean 2006 lore of "천적/먹이사슬" (who holds the upper hand): A형 feels O형 is "언제나 만만한 상대"; O형 "A형 앞에 나가면 압박감"; O형 is B형's "천적" ("절대강자 B형에게는 천적이다"); B형 handles A형 "어렵지 않게"; "B형에게 약한 AB형"; "AB형에게 휘둘리는 A형"; betrayal chain O→B, B→A, A→O hurts most, reverse direction is dropped "미련 없이" — [m.cafe.daum.net/latindance/73e/16777](https://m.cafe.daum.net/latindance/73e/16777)

### Gaps

## Key Question 4: Does popular lore distinguish friendship from romantic compatibility?

### Takeaway
Korean circulated tables are framed as 연애/남녀 궁합 and do not provide a separate friendship grid; the only friendship-specific ranking found (Japanese, AB-centered) shows friendship favors same-type/similar-temperament pairs while romance favors complementary pairs, and is non-directional (same-sex pairs).

### Cited Findings
- Korean cafe table #2 explicitly says O남×A여 "친구로도 연인으로도 잘 맞는다", and AB남×O여 is "편한 대화 상대는 되어도 연애에는 불안정" — the only cells where friend vs lover is contrasted — [m.cafe.daum.net/urisi/EyEr/1531](https://m.cafe.daum.net/urisi/EyEr/1531)
- 레이디경향 16-combination article is written for romance; AB남+O여 is described as feeling like "친구" at times and "애인" at others — [lady.khan.co.kr/finance/article/6764](https://lady.khan.co.kr/finance/article/6764)
- Japanese AB-type article gives separate lists: romance (directional: AB男 best = A女 > AB女 > B女 > O女; AB女 best = B男 > AB男 > A男 > O男) vs friendship (same-sex, single list: AB女×AB女 > AB男×A男 > AB女×A女 > AB男×AB男 > AB女×B女); conclusion "romance favors complementary types, friendship favors shared understanding" — [smartlog.jp/152178](https://smartlog.jp/152178)
- Smartlog's 10 symmetric pair descriptions are framed as general/work/friend relationships, separate from its directional romance ranking — [smartlog.jp/149249](https://smartlog.jp/149249)
- Korean portal article lists separate "friend" vs "partner" preferences per type (e.g., A "노력하는" friend, "주도면밀하고 끈기가 있는" partner) but its pair-compatibility question was not split by relationship type — [kidd.co.kr/news/161658](https://kidd.co.kr/news/161658)
- Korean search for "혈액형 친구 궁합" returned only romance-centered tables; no Korean friendship-specific 4×4 table was found — [search result summary: jab-guyver.co.kr/94](https://jab-guyver.co.kr/94), [m.cafe.daum.net/urisi/EyEr/1531](https://m.cafe.daum.net/urisi/EyEr/1531)

### Inferences
- For a friendship/general-compatibility app, the safest approach is the symmetric (unordered) table built from the Korean % tables, optionally with a small same-type bonus (+5) in "friend mode" reflecting the Japanese finding that friendship favors similar temperament.

### Gaps
- No Korean source provides a full friendship-only 4×4 grid; the friendship nuance is supported by a single Japanese AB-centered article.

## Key Question 5: Symmetric vs directional; Korean vs Japanese versions; Rh factor

### Takeaway
Korean circulated tables are directional (남×여) with different numbers for mirrored pairs, while Japanese "general" pair descriptions are symmetric and only romance rankings are directional; the lore is Japanese in origin (Furukawa 1927, Nomi 1971 『血液型でわかる相性』), reached Korea via translated books in the 1980s, and in both countries ignores Rh (which was discovered after the theory formed).

### Cited Findings
- Korean tables: mirrored cells differ (e.g., O남×A여 95% vs A남×O여 90%; B남×A여 20% vs A남×B여 25%; AB남×O여 30% vs O남×AB여 35%) — [jab-guyver.co.kr/94](https://jab-guyver.co.kr/94); (O남×A여 95% vs A남×O여 80%; B남×AB여 90% vs AB남×B여 60%) — [m.cafe.daum.net/urisi/EyEr/1531](https://m.cafe.daum.net/urisi/EyEr/1531)
- 레이디경향 article: "Each pair is labeled 남/여, and mirrored pairs get separate, different advice"; only AB+AB is framed "남녀를 따지지 않는다면" — [lady.khan.co.kr/finance/article/6764](https://lady.khan.co.kr/finance/article/6764)
- Japanese: 10 unordered pairs for general relations + directional 男性×女性 romance ranking — [smartlog.jp/149249](https://smartlog.jp/149249); jobcannon lists pairs as unordered — [jobcannon.io/ja/blood-type-personality](https://jobcannon.io/ja/blood-type-personality)
- Korean survey treats pairs as unordered hyphenated combinations (A–O, B–O, A–B…) — [kidd.co.kr/news/161658](https://kidd.co.kr/news/161658)
- Origin: Furukawa Takeji 1927 "血液型による気質の研究", 1932 『血液型と気質』; Nomi Masahiko (TV scriptwriter, not scientist) 1971 『血液型でわかる相性』 popularized it; boom 1970s–early 2000s; theory is common "日本とその影響を受けた韓国など一部地域だけ" — [ja.wikipedia.org 血液型性格分類](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/血液型性格分類)
- Korea adopted it in the 1980s through Japanese books/magazines "often translated without permission"; best-known title 《X형 인간의 미학》; Nomi's 《혈액형으로 알 수 있는 상성》 named — [namu.wiki/w/혈액형 성격설](https://namu.wiki/w/혈액형%20성격설)
- Korean cafe-circulated "혈액형 연애 성공법" (동서고금) is the credited source of 레이디경향's 16-combination guide — [lady.khan.co.kr/finance/article/6764](https://lady.khan.co.kr/finance/article/6764)
- Korea/Japan status: Japanese broadcasters dropped blood-type segments around 2008 but it remains in daily life; in Korea belief declined since 2010s and MBTI replaced it in celebrity profiles in the 2020s; 2017 Gallup Korea: 58% believe blood type affects personality, ~22% consider it when choosing a partner; 2021 Hankook Research: 56% — [namu.wiki/w/혈액형 성격설](https://namu.wiki/w/혈액형%20성격설)
- Korea-specific negativity toward B형: B is the least-preferred spouse type; film 《B형 남자친구》 is listed among blood-type themed works — [namu.wiki/w/혈액형 성격설](https://namu.wiki/w/혈액형%20성격설); minority types B and AB "are more likely to be negatively stereotyped"; "bura-hara" (blood harassment) reported in Japan and Korea — [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_type_personality_theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_type_personality_theory)
- Japanese population share A≈40%, O≈30%, B≈20%, AB≈10% (日本血液製剤協会 via Oggi); Oggi notes A's population share may skew "most compatible" survey results — [oggi.jp/6540964](https://oggi.jp/6540964)
- Rh: Rh system discovered in 1940, after the 1920s–30s period when the theory took shape; the lore uses ABO only — [namu.wiki/w/혈액형 성격설](https://namu.wiki/w/혈액형%20성격설). No Korean or Japanese 궁합표 consulted mentions Rh — [jab-guyver.co.kr/94](https://jab-guyver.co.kr/94), [smartlog.jp/149249](https://smartlog.jp/149249), [ja.wikipedia.org 血液型性格分類](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/血液型性格分類)
- Scientific status: 2014 Duo (결혼정보회사) data on 3,000 couples: same-type A–A couples 42% (largest) but counts match population shares, so blood type had no effect on marriage — [sidae.com 2014](https://www.sidae.com/article/2014101315028012098); [en.wikipedia.org](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_type_personality_theory)
- Korea/Japan "혈액형별 인성과 궁합" booklets appeared in 1927 and 1971; Korean belief sometimes attributed to "일제의 잔재" (unconfirmed) — [ppss.kr/archives/24981](https://ppss.kr/archives/24981)

### Inferences
- The core consensus (A–O best, A–B worst, AB–AB good, O–O clash) is identical in Korea and Japan; the observable Korean differences are (1) tables are packaged as 남×여 % grids, (2) harsher scoring of B-type cells (B남×A여 as low as 20%), (3) stronger stigma on B형, (4) B–AB rated higher in Korea than in Japanese romance rankings.
- For the app: store the directional 4×4 and compute a symmetric score as the mean of the two directions when sex is unknown; always ignore Rh (ABO only).

### Gaps
- Could not confirm from a fetched page the release year of 《B형 남자친구》 (commonly 2005) — Namu Wiki lists the title only.
- Japanese Wikipedia has no compatibility table; Namu Wiki has no 궁합 table either, so no "encyclopedic" canonical table exists in either language.
